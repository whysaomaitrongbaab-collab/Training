### 3. Mistakes, Regressions & Corrections
- First answer had 3 errors, found by reading the Civil Code PDF Makham supplied (`D:\000download\ประมวลกฎหมายแพ่งและพาณิชย์ พ.ศ. 2535.pdf`, Krisdika compiled text, last amendment noted in it = Act No. 19 of 2008, doc dated 21 Mar 2012; text extracted to scratchpad, not copied into a repo):
  - Minors: wrote "may be terminated"; s.21 says the act is voidable (โมฆียะ), s.175/176/177-181 give annulment, restitution, ratification only after majority, 1-year / 10-year limits.
  - Compromise (s.850-852): wrote "must be in writing and signed"; s.851 only says no suit to enforce without signed written evidence from the liable side; s.850 needs mutual concession.
  - Co-ownership (s.1357-1363): "sale needs everyone" is only true for the whole asset (s.1361 para 2); own share can be disposed freely; unequal shares need to be written (s.1357 presumes equal); no-partition agreement capped at 10 years at a time (s.1363).
