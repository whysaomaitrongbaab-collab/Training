# AI catalog — alternative 100-200B Vision models (other than Qwen)

*Compiled Aug 26, 2026 from the research in `2026-08-25.md` / `2026-08-25(teach mk).md` / `2026-08-26.md`
Criteria: size 100-200B (± loosely) · not Qwen in brand or backbone · commercial use allowed ·
a LoRA training tool confirmed to really work (not just claimed in the docs)*

**What we use now:** `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` (35B, Apache-2.0, already being trained in this project)

## The verdict

**The only one that passes every condition (size + license + genuinely trainable): Llama-4-Scout**
Runner-up: GLM-4.6V (license passes, but stuck on a bug that blocks training for now)

**Nobody has tested yet how well any of them reads Thai** — recommend a zero-shot Thai-OCR test
before deciding to invest in renting a real GPU for any of them

**Why they're hard to find:** most truly open (Apache/MIT) 100-200B vision models on the market right now
**borrow their brain from Qwen** (both InternVL models) — the ones with no Qwen in them at all usually come with licenses
that carry hidden conditions (Pixtral, NVLM, MiniMax), except Meta's Llama family, which is the most open
